# ADR: Decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: Record when the decision is made
- Deciders: Name accountable roles

## Context

Describe the durable technical question and the constraints that make it significant.

## Decision

State the chosen direction after it is accepted. While unresolved, describe the proposed direction without marking it accepted.

## Consequences

List benefits, costs, operational obligations, migration impact, and known limitations.

## Alternatives

Summarize the serious options that were rejected and why.

## Compliance and verification

Explain how future changes and tests can demonstrate that the decision remains enforced.
